Support

We usually reply within 2 business days.

Need help with Foodoo? Email us at phvzed@gmail.com and include your iPhone model, iOS version and, if it's about a product, its barcode. The answers below cover the most common questions.

01 How does the health score work?

Each product gets a score from 0 to 100 based on its nutritional quality (Nutri-Score), its additives and their risk levels, and organic certification. Tap the ⓘ next to a score in the app to see the full method. Personalized warnings come from your answers to the health quiz and don't change the score.

02 A product is missing or its details look wrong

Product data comes from Open Food Facts, an open database anyone can improve. You can add or correct a product there, and Foodoo picks up the change. If a score still looks wrong, email us the barcode.

03 Free scans and Premium

Your first 5 scans are free. Premium unlocks unlimited scans, with a weekly plan or a yearly plan that starts with a 3-day free trial. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

04 Restore a purchase on a new device

Sign in with the same Apple Account you subscribed with, open the subscription screen in Foodoo and tap Restore.

05 Cancel your subscription or get a refund

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Foodoo and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

06 Your data

Your quiz answers and scan history are stored on your device. Deleting the app removes them. See our Privacy Policy for details.

07 Is Foodoo medical advice?

No. Foodoo provides general nutrition information. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian about your health conditions, allergies or diet.
